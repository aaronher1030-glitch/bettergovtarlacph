# Coordination Plan

This plan prepares a constructive discussion. It does not send messages or imply that any stakeholder has agreed to participate.

## Stakeholders to map

| Stakeholder | Why involve them | Appropriate first ask |
|---|---|---|
| Municipal Information / IT / website administrator | Canonical pages, accessibility, current links, publishing workflow | Identify official sources and how corrections are handled |
| Municipal Budget Office | Budget document names, fiscal years, terminology | Confirm definitions and public source locations |
| Municipal Accounting / Treasurer | Distinguish accounting/disbursement records and public reports | Identify publicly released reports and context |
| Municipal Planning and Development Office | Plans, annual investment program, project monitoring sources | Clarify public planning/project records and geographic coverage |
| Bids and Awards Committee / General Services Office | Procurement source links and stage definitions | Map bid notice, award, contract, and delivery records |
| Sangguniang Bayan Secretariat | Ordinances, resolutions, legislative records | Confirm official searchable archive or canonical source |
| DILG field office / FDP focal person | Full Disclosure Policy source and posting periods | Point to public filings and completeness expectations |
| BetterGov maintainers | Template, API limits, contribution/review route | Confirm current setup, data interpretation, and listing process |
| Residents / civil society / local researchers | Prioritize findability and test usability | Identify records residents struggle to locate |

Contact relevant offices through public official channels. Keep messages factual, short, and focused on feedback. Do not ask individual staff to share restricted information.

## Meeting sequence

### 1. Internal preparation

- Review this concept note and source checklist.
- Build a source inventory using public links only.
- Prepare a 3–5 minute demo flow with a few verified records.
- Make an explicit list of uncertainties and questions.

### 2. Introductory LGU discussion

Suggested agenda (30–45 minutes):
1. Introductions and project status: independent concept, not official.
2. Show the public-record discovery problem and a small demo.
3. Ask which information is most useful and which office owns each source.
4. Discuss data interpretation, stale links, and correction routing.
5. Agree on one narrow pilot and what must be verified.
6. Record next steps, responsible party, and date; no assumption of partnership.

### 3. BetterGov technical discussion

- Confirm the current transparency-portal template and API terms/limitations.
- Ask how municipal matching, place names, and exclusions should be documented.
- Confirm the exact directory steps and whether a Concepcion listing exists now.
- Ask about accessibility, branding, source attribution, and maintenance expectations.

### 4. Review and decision

- Share a demo and source register for correction.
- Resolve disputed or ambiguous data before publication.
- Choose a maintenance owner and cadence.
- Decide whether the project remains an independent directory, becomes an LGU-supported site, or pauses.

## Demo presentation outline

1. **Problem:** Public information exists in many pages and files; residents may have to know where to look.
2. **Walkthrough:** Search a service or record, see the source owner/date, open the original.
3. **Transparency:** Show missing/uncertain sources and correction process.
4. **Budget view (if validated):** Show amount type, fiscal year, original document, and limits.
5. **Request:** Ask for feedback, source ownership, and a pilot contact—not formal endorsement on the spot.
